    Tips

    • Crop out your name, student number and photo.
    • On a phone, use Desktop site or landscape so the table fits.
    • Use screenshots, not photos of a screen.
    • PNG, JPG, WEBP or PDF · up to 6 files.

    AI or Offline?

    AI modeMost accurate

    Reads any format, even calendar view. Needs your own API key. Google Gemini's is free.

    Offline modeNo key

    Reads on your device. Less accurate, so double-check. COR PDF or List view only.

    Get a free Gemini key

    1. Open Google AI Studio and sign in.
    2. Tap Create API key and copy it.
    3. Paste it here. Keep it private like a password.

    Your privacy

    • Nothing is recorded. Everything runs in your browser. ASCEND has no server and doesn't collect, upload, or store your files, schedule, or key.
    • Hide personal info. Crop out your name, student number, photo, address, birthday, contacts, fees and QR codes. Only the subject table and term are needed.
    • Offline mode keeps your file on your device.
    • AI mode sends your images straight to the AI company you pick (Google, OpenAI or Anthropic), using your own key and under their terms.
    • Your key stays in this tab unless you tick Remember, which saves it in this browser only. Tap Forget to remove it.
    • Closing or refreshing the page clears your schedule. Downloads are saved only where you choose.
    • Fonts and readers load from public CDNs (Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr). They never receive your files.
