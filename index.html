ASCEND Schedule Viewer Academic Society of Computer Engineering Discipline

Turn your COR into a schedule you can set as your wallpaper.

Upload your Certificate of Registration or portal schedule, check the details, then download a clean image sized for your phone.

Your privacy comes first

  • Nothing is recorded. This tool runs entirely in your browser. ASCEND does not collect, upload, or store your files, your schedule, or your API key.
  • Please hide personal details before uploading: your name, student number, photo, address, birthday, contact info, fees, and QR codes or barcodes. Only the subject table and the term or school year are needed.
  • Offline mode reads your file on your own device. AI mode sends your images straight from your browser to the AI company you pick, using your own key and under that company's terms.
Read the full privacy notice

What this tool does with your data. Your uploaded files are read in this browser tab only. The extracted schedule lives in the tab's memory and disappears when you close or refresh the page. ASCEND has no server, database, analytics, or tracking for this tool.

AI mode. When you use AI mode, your images are sent directly from your device to the provider you selected (Google, OpenAI, or Anthropic) with your own API key, so they can read the schedule. That provider handles the images under its own privacy terms. ASCEND never receives them. Use Offline mode if you'd rather nothing leaves your device.

Your API key. Your key is kept in memory for this tab only. If you tick Remember on this device, it is saved in this browser's local storage so you don't need to paste it again. You can clear it at any time with Forget key. Never share your key with anyone.

Downloads. The schedule image and the optional .json backup are created on your device and saved only where you choose.

Third-party files. Fonts, the PDF reader, and the offline text recognizer are downloaded from public CDNs (Google Fonts, cdnjs, jsDelivr). Like any website, those servers see a normal download request, but they never receive your files or schedule.

Please don't upload personal information. Crop or cover your name, student number, photo, address, birthday, contact details, assessment or fees, and QR codes or barcodes. The tool does not need them.

Upload your schedule

What to upload for the best results

  1. Best
    Your COR as a PDF, downloaded from the student portal. Both modes read it very well.
  2. Great
    A screenshot of “My Schedule Viewer” in List view (tap the ☰ list button). Make sure every subject row and the title with the term and AY are visible.
  3. AI only
    Calendar or grid view screenshots. If you needed more than one screenshot, upload all of them.

Tips

  • Crop to the schedule table plus the term or AY heading.
  • Cover or crop out your name, student number, photo, and other personal details.
  • On a phone, switch on Desktop site or turn the phone sideways so the whole table fits. Zoom in until the text is sharp.
  • Use real screenshots, not photos of a screen. They're far more accurate.
  • PNG, JPG, WEBP, or PDF, up to 6 files.
